LEG 2 — SEC EDGAR 8-K ENGINE: the one that survives the gate
================================================================================================================
  Equities have no pre-close mechanism. A market-moving 8-K hits EDGAR while the stock is
  continuously tradeable, so unlike Kalshi macro there IS a window by construction. That
  makes it the better leg, and `special_sits/edgar_scanner.py` already has the fetch,
  backoff and rate-limit discipline to build on.
  The binding constraint is different and must be measured first: EDGAR's own dissemination
  lag. Filings appear in the full-text index minutes to hours after acceptance, and the
  paid direct feeds that professionals use are the reason. Before writing a parser, measure
  acceptance-timestamp vs index-availability on real 8-Ks — that delta is the whole edge and
  it is not something a faster poller can shrink.
  Second constraint, worth knowing now: free intraday equity history is ~7 days of 1-minute
  bars, so the reaction study has to run forward from today rather than backward over a year.
